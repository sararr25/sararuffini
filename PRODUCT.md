# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, agencies, brands, and collaborators evaluating Sara Ruffini's work. They arrive to understand her range quickly, watch the showreel, explore selected projects, and decide whether her visual voice fits a commission or collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Present Sara as a distinctive video editor and multidisciplinary creative through a portfolio that demonstrates craft before explaining it. Success means visitors feel her pace, personality, and range immediately, then move naturally into project work, the about page, or contact.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, quick-witted, and kinetic. The experience should feel personal and handmade, with the confidence and timing of a strong edit rather than the polish of a generic agency template.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate portfolios built from uniform card grids.
- Quiet editorial minimalism that suppresses Sara's playful, collage-led identity.
- Motion-heavy showcase sites that delay navigation, hijack scrolling, or animate every element equally.
- Soft, glossy UI treatments that replace the site's hard borders, cut-paper shapes, and high-contrast color.

## Design Principles

- Edit for impact: concentrate motion and contrast around a few memorable beats.
- Show the work early: keep the showreel and featured projects central to the first visit.
- Make craft visible: use tactile layers, drawn marks, and collage composition as evidence of a hands-on process.
- Keep play legible: expressive composition must preserve readable copy, clear links, and reliable navigation.
- Let interaction reveal personality: movement should reward attention without becoming an obstacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for public-facing content. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic link labels, sufficient contrast, and an immediate non-animated experience for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Smooth scrolling must retain native scrolling behavior and remain disabled for reduced-motion users.
